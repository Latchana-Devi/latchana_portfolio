EV Charging Station Management

STM32 Voltage / Current Sensors Relay Modules UART I2C ThingSpeak Embedded C

Developed an intelligent electric vehicle charging monitoring system using STM32 microcontroller and Embedded C to oversee charging cycles, manage current/voltage dynamics, and prevent overcharge conditions.

Key Metrics & Benchmarks:
  • Sensors polled and read every 100 ms with calibration achieving ±2% accuracy.
  • Achieved a 15% efficiency improvement across battery charging cycles.
  • Telemetry updates streamed securely to ThingSpeak cloud every 15 seconds.
